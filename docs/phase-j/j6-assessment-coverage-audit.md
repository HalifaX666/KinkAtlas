# Phase J.6 assessment coverage audit

## Verified coverage

- Role library: 1,107 roles; 1,107 reviewed definitions; 0 unavailable.
- Scored role model: 101 roles using 44 traits.
- Discovery bank: 72 questions (20 broad and 52 adaptive).
- Production limit: 26 Discovery questions maximum.
- Expanded production-selector audit: 52/52 adaptive questions reachable through valid journeys; all ten questions absent from the original fixture corpus were reached.
- Refine bank: 28 questions; at most six are presented, while semantic evidence eligibility is uncapped and deterministic.
- Trait diagnostics: zero under-covered role-used traits and zero mechanically correlated pairs. Every role-used trait has more than one reachable question in the coverage test.
- Calibration: 100/100 personas, 7/7 adaptive discrimination scenarios, and 10/10 decision pathways pass.

The 49 questions with uniform trait groups across scored answers are a diagnostic about question structure, not evidence that choices use one generic intensity. `calculateTraitScores()` reads each selected answer's individual `effects`; `noScore` choices do not contribute.

## Evidence and distinction findings

- Core, differentiating, and contrary traits are calculated independently. Contrary evidence reduces alignment only when observed; missing evidence does not.
- Authority-sensitive roles use explicit qualification evidence. The Authority Holder/Dominant pair remains the closest scored pair (about 0.92 similarity), but `r-authority-style`, authority qualification, structure, and responsibility evidence provide measurable discrimination.
- The seven adaptive calibration comparisons all select the more discriminating question: authority neighbors, rope direction, service position, praise direction, objectification direction, primal direction, and authority versus activity position.
- Neutral/unanswered/prefer-not states consistently add no positive evidence. The sparse browser journey produces no fabricated primary.
- Confidence is based on relevant answered questions and weighted theme coverage. The calibration diagnostic shows question-count and contributing-effect counts can differ; 29 hypothetical band crossings were observed under a different unapproved counting rule. This is a documented confidence-policy question, not a reproduced defect in the current contract.
- Question order is deterministic. Expanded journeys prove reachability, but they do not prove equal real-world exposure frequency; production telemetry is intentionally absent.

## Conclusion

All scored roles and traits have a valid evidence path, and every adaptive question is reachable. The nine family gaps remain correctly separated into intentional editorial coverage, gated exact-vocabulary coverage, genuine design candidates, and taxonomy decisions. No scoring or question change is authorized by this audit.
